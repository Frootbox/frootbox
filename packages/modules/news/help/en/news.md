**News** keeps your articles: announcements, press releases or blog
posts. Each article has a date, a title, a short teaser, a text and,
if you like, an image and categories.

## Show the news on the website

A page shows the news when an admin chooses it in its settings under
**This page shows**, e.g. on a page called "News". The articles appear
there, newest first, page by page, between the blocks of the page. Each
article gets its own address below that page, e.g.
`/news/new-varieties`, and only the article shows there.

For separate news, such as press and blog, an admin adds another
**space** there. Each has its own articles and its own page; under
**News** you choose it as a tab at the top.

The block **Newest articles** shows the latest few, e.g. on the start
page, from all spaces or from one, with a link to all of them.

## Write an article

Choose **New article**, write the title and the text and save. The
article stays a draft until you choose **Publish**. Changes to a
published article are a draft as well, visitors see them once you
publish again.

Under **Plan** you set a time when the draft goes online by itself, or
when the article goes offline.

## Categories

Under **Categories** you create topics such as "Events" or "Press",
also below each other. Visitors can filter the list by them. Deleting a
category keeps its articles.

## Tags

Tick the tags that fit or type new words, separated by commas. The
article shows its tags at the end, and each leads to the list of all
articles with that tag. A teaser of the newest articles can show only
those with one tag.

## Feed

The page with the list offers an RSS feed of the newest articles, so
people can follow your news in a feed reader.
