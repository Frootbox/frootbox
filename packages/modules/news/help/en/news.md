**News** keeps your articles: announcements, press releases or blog
posts. Each article has a date, a title, a short teaser, a text and,
if you like, an image and categories.

## Show the news on the website

Add the block **News** to a page, e.g. one called "News". It lists the
articles, newest first, page by page. Each article gets its own address
below that page, e.g. `/news/new-varieties`, and the block shows the
article there.

If several pages have the block, the first one in the sitemap holds the
addresses; the others link to it.

The block **Newest articles** shows the latest few, e.g. on the start
page, with a link to all of them.

## Write an article

Choose **New article**, write the title and the text and save. The
article stays a draft until you choose **Publish**. Changes to a
published article are a draft as well, visitors see them once you
publish again.

Under **Plan** you set a time when the draft goes online by itself, or
when the article goes offline.

## Categories

Under **Categories** you create topics such as "Events" or "Press".
Visitors can filter the list by them. Deleting a category keeps its
articles.

## Feed

The page with the list offers an RSS feed of the newest articles, so
people can follow your news in a feed reader.
