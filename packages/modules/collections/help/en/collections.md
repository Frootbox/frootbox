Under **Collections** you keep entries of the same kind, such as
references, projects or show homes. Each collection has its own tab,
each entry a title, a short teaser for the cards and search engines, a
text and an image.

## Name the collections

The first collection is called like the module until an admin names it
under **Spaces**, e.g. "References". There an admin also adds further
collections, e.g. "Projects" next to "References".

## Create an entry

Choose the tab of the collection and **New entry**, then give it a title.
The entry stays a draft until you choose **Publish**. Under **Times** you
set when it goes online and when it goes offline by itself.

The **Position** sets the order: smaller numbers come first, with the
same number the newest comes first. Leave it at 0 and the newest is at
the top.

## Show them on the website

A page shows a collection when an admin chooses it in its settings under
**This page shows**. There the entries stand as cards with image, title
and teaser. If the entries have different categories or tags, visitors
can filter by them. Each entry gets its own address below this page,
e.g. `/references/house-by-the-lake`.

In the editing mode, choose a **Form for requests** from **Forms** in the
settings of the list. It appears on each entry, and each submission
names the entry under **Regarding**.

The block **Entries of a collection** shows a few entries, e.g. the
newest references on the start page, of all collections or of one,
optionally only of a category or with a tag, with a link to all.

## Categories and tags

Under **Categories** you add areas, e.g. "Residential" or "Commercial",
also below each other. Tick the fitting tags or type new words,
separated by commas. Visitors can filter the list by both.

## Fields of a collection

Admins choose **Fields** above the entries of a collection and add what
the entries have in common, e.g. "Area" as a number in m², "Year" as a
date or "Construction" as a choice from a list. There are texts, long
texts, numbers with a unit, dates, yes or no, links, choices, images
and files. Editors fill them in under **More fields**; the page of an
entry shows the filled ones as a fact sheet below the image.

Name and kind of a field stay. If a field goes, the values in the
entries stay saved and come back with a new field of the same name.
For a choice, write one option per line. A changed line keeps the
values of the entries as long as the number of lines stays the same.

A theme can add further fields to the entries, too. They appear in the
form under **More fields** and in the editing mode right on the page.
